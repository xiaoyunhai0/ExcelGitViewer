## English

### Resizable review workspace

- Redesigned commits, changed files, worksheet changes, cell differences, and before/after context
  as compact review tables.
- Added horizontal and vertical splitters so every review area can be resized.
- Highlights the selected old cell in red, the new cell in green, and matching row/column headers
  in amber.
- Persists splitter proportions and restores the layout after restart.
- Improved loading, empty, error, and selection states while background work is running.

## 简体中文

### 可自由缩放的审查工作区

- 将提交、变化文件、工作表变化、单元格差异和修改前后上下文重做为紧凑审查表格。
- 增加横向和纵向分隔条，所有审查区域均可调整大小。
- 当前旧单元格使用红色、新单元格使用绿色，对应行列标题使用黄色定位。
- 自动保存区域比例，重启后恢复上次布局。
- 优化后台任务执行期间的加载、空数据、错误和选择状态。
